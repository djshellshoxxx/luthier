# Luthier Wiki

Luthier is a physically modelled guitar plugin (VST3, AU, CLAP, standalone; JUCE 8). Notes are
produced by digital waveguide strings, not samples.

- [User Guide](User-Guide) - install, load, play, presets
- [Developer Guide](Developer-Guide) - build, test, editions, conventions
- [Architecture and Design Rationale](Architecture-and-Design-Rationale) - why the engine is built this way
- [Codebase Map](Codebase-Map) - where to find what

Authoritative sources: `spec/README.md` (overview), `spec/INDEX.md` (all specs), and
`spec/DECISIONS.md` (judgement calls). Where these wiki pages and the spec disagree, the spec wins.
