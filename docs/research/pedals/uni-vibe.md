## Signal chain, stage by stage
Corroborated only (no invented values):
- Four discrete-transistor phase-shift stages, no op-amps; one incandescent lamp driven by LFO optically couples to four LDRs (DAFx 2019).
- Phasing capacitor values deliberately unequal ('almost arbitrary'); actual values not visible in snippets. A forum note: 1uF series caps with LDRs affect bass.
