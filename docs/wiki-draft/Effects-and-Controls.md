# Effects and Controls

Two separate lists exist, and [EFFECTS_REFERENCE.md](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/luthier/blob/master/docs/EFFECTS_REFERENCE.md) keeps them apart:

- the 13 registered **pedalboard models** (10 generic effects and 3 prototypes), and
- the 23 **legacy rack pedal types** in the two eight-slot racks of the Advanced panel.

Each table gives every control with its range, unit and default, read from the source. Other controls (header, Easy and Advanced modes, amp, cabinet, room, options) are described in the [User manual](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/luthier/blob/master/docs/USER_MANUAL.md); keyboard shortcuts are in [KEYBOARD_SHORTCUTS.md](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/luthier/blob/master/docs/KEYBOARD_SHORTCUTS.md).
