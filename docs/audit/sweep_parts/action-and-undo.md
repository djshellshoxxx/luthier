## action-and-undo.md

Undo is now the class/target/timestamp/boundary stack in `Support/UndoHistory` (200 entries, 200 ms grouping, boundaries, Ctrl-Y, Ctrl-Alt-Z with a banner, the History dropdown with search, the Show undo depth footer and the family-switch warning) with entries pushed from mod-matrix, snapshot, setlist, MIDI Learn, pedal, practice, character, routing, rhythm and IR edits, and about 45 tests in UndoTests/UndoCoverageTests. Left: snapshot rearrange has no UI, a drag that pauses stays one entry by design, choice-parameter grouping and the Ctrl-Alt-Z banner have no test.
