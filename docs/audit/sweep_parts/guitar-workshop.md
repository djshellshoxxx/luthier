## guitar-workshop.md

The parts model is essentially DONE: `Part`, `PartLibrary` and `WorkshopGuitar`, resolution and fallback, compatibility warnings, string-count clamping, Save As Guitar/Part, the preset override and parameter retirement are implemented, reachable from the WORKSHOP bench and tested. Remaining gaps: four slots (top, fretboard, tailpiece, pickguard) have no drawer category, there is no finish or hardware editor, no folder watcher, the missing-part banner has no jump action, the inspector does not show the string-count excess, bundle export is untested, and the per-slot rendered-spectrum test is missing.
