## qa-polish.md

This spec is a ship-gate checklist, and a large part of it is process: the host and platform matrix, the bug bash, videos, legal sign-off, the final human check and post-release monitoring. That part is still open. Since the baseline, the integration branch has gained CI on Linux, Windows and macOS: unit tests, pluginval at strictness 5 on push and 10 nightly, auval and clap-validator. It also has installers for all three platforms and a placeholder EULA. The automated robustness and QA suite is now merged (re-verified): the 100k fuzz, the state fuzz, boot and memory probes, stress tests, byte-flip corruption, pedal/amp/realism QA tests, per-panel "?", screenshots in every palette, reflow at every scale, NEW dots, THIRD_PARTY_LICENCES and file-open. Still missing: the golden-render suite, host-format switching, CPU-stress preset switching, shipped translations, a documentation/screenshot pipeline and the performance numbers at spec level (idle 1.5%).
